This example demonstrates the UConn Department of Statistics letterhead.
The logo and sender information form the masthead, while the recipient,
salutation, body, and closing follow in a consistent layout.

To write your own letter, edit the sender settings in
\texttt{\string\UConnLHsetup} at the top of this file, then replace the
recipient and body. No separate sender setup file is required.

Compile the letter with \texttt{pdflatex SampleLetter.tex}. The
\texttt{signed} option includes your configured signature image; remove
that option for a letter with space for a handwritten signature.

Longer letters continue on additional pages with the sender name, date,
and page number. The closing and signature stay together.
